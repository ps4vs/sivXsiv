# Know Your PlayGround

CAPITALISM REWARDS RARE AND VALUABLE
	You need to stay at intersection 
	- what you are really good at, and 
	- what people desire, and 
	- what other companies are not good at doing
	  
BUSSINESS EXIST TO FULFIL HUMAN DESIRES
	Underlying desires are the same
	- capitalism fules old things done in better way, not shiny new things
	- nothing is enough for humans, desires evolve into derivatives
	  
EVIDENCE OF DESIRE IS IN PEOPLE's BEHAVIOUR (AND NOT IN WHAT THEY SAY)
- biggest mistake you can make is believe what people say, instead of observing what people do.
- behavior not words is the real evidence
  
NO MARKET IS EVER DONE
- find a better way to solve underlying human desire, people will move, no loyolty
- solutions come and go, but desires always stay
